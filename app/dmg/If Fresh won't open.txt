Installing Fresh

1. Drag Fresh onto the Applications folder next to it.
2. Open Fresh from Applications.

The first time, macOS says it can't verify Fresh, because it isn't sold through Apple.
Click Done, then:

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
4. Scroll down to "Fresh was blocked" and click Open Anyway.
5. Enter your password. From then on Fresh opens normally.

Fresh looks through your home folder for things you can clean up, so it works best with
Full Disk Access: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Full Disk Access, then add Fresh.
Nothing is ever deleted outright; everything goes to the Trash, and Undo puts it back.
